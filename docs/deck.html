The problem

Every product sold in the EU needs a passport.
Nobody trusts the person retyping it.

Suppliers send spec sheets, test reports, certificates — each in its own layout. The data ships as PDFs a person retypes into the passport, and downstream nobody knows how far to trust the result.
Batteries: EU passport from Feb 2027 · Textiles: ~2029
Anti-greenwashing rules apply since 27 Sep 2026 (Directive 2024/825) — claims need sourced evidence now, not at the deadline.
Existing AI extraction is fast but scored on vibes — the error risk lands silently on the buyer, and usually surfaces via an auditor. No accountability layer.

What we built

Passport data where every answer is priced.

1Extract
A chain of independent Sieves, each a different AI Worker with a different view of the same Document — original PDF, Markdown, checked Markmarkdown, tie-break on disagreement.
Unclear → Not stated, with quotes. Not a guess.
2Bond
On delivery the Operator locks a 10 USDC Bond + the Brand pays a 1 USDC Fee in escrow on Stellar. The escrow also holds a hash of the delivered Fields.
Hash in escrow → the answer can't quietly change later.
3Challenge
During the Challenge Window anyone cites the page and quote that says otherwise. A neutral Arbiter decides from the Document alone.
Upheld → 1/10 of the Bond slashed to the Brand. Automatic.
The Bond doesn't make the model careful. It makes careless Pipelines expensive to run, and their error rate public.

Grounded in research

Four findings, four design decisions.

I'll pay you or I'll kill you — tips and threats generally have no significant effect on benchmark performance (Meincke, Mollick et al. 2025 · 7,450 trials)
→
We don't claim stakes make models smarter. The Bond doesn't buy accuracy — it turns confidence into a price and makes the error rate public.
Financial agents ordered to survive faked reports in up to 80% of runs under loss pressure (Lu et al. 2026, arXiv 2603.05028 · small-n case study)
→
The stake never enters the prompt. Plain code — not the model — computes locks and slashes; Workers only see Fields and quotes.
Raw AI confidence signals collapse toward all-positive; disagreement between two structurally different extraction passes reaches ROC AUC 0.928 on document fields (EXTRACTCONF, Kumar 2026)
→
The Sieve chain, not stated confidence, drives routing: independent views disagree usefully; disagreement gets the tie-break, then a human.
Programmatic agent-insurance detection rises from 42.2% to 83.0% when agents emit structured, checkable evidence (Hua et al., CMU 2026, arXiv 2610.18230 · closest prior art)
→
Every Field carries page + quote. Evidence a Challenge can cite and an Arbiter can check — accuracy decided from the Document alone.
No prior work we found stakes per Field on extraction accuracy — nearest are EXTRACTCONF (confidence only) and Verifiable Agent Insurance (policy violations, Ethereum L2). All four findings are in the repo's research notes, caveats included.

Why Stellar

Verification you don't have to ask us for.

ESCROW & SLASH
Soroban escrow (Trustless Work) with rule-based, automatic settlement. Fees per Field are fractions of a cent — per-field money works for the first time.
TAMPER-EVIDENT
The escrow holds a hash of the delivered Fields. Changing the answer after approval means a new version — and the hash move is on the record.
TRACK RECORD
Bonds, hashes and Slashes sit on a public ledger that doubles as each Operator's reputation — nobody has to take the platform's word for it.
FILES ELSEWHERE
Documents live on ICP (Origyn's storage, already in production). All the money logic lives on Stellar. One chain per job.

Where this goes

An open market of document Operators.

The workforce is open infrastructure: any AI agent on MCP can claim a Document and deliver a Passport, with its own Bond and its own track record. Today there are two operators; the design wants many.
Live today
Testnet escrow, real extraction, Challenges on the same Documents, Slashes on stellar.expert. Judges' agents already connect via MCP — as Workers or outside Operators.
Per-Field stakes
Field-level Bonds priced to the risk of a wrong answer: honest confidence earns, loud confidence costs. Same mechanics, finer grain.
Brands never touch the chain
In production Brands pay by invoice; the escrow settles behind a licensed partner. EU battery passports from Feb 2027, textiles around 2029 — the demand is dated.
Why us
Origyn is already a Digital Product Passport platform (ICP, role-based Files); HackMeridian added the accountability layer. An outside Operator was successfully delivered against in a test run.
